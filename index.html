02 — How a project runs

No black box.
You see it take shape.

A clear path from first conversation to a system your team relies on, with a working build to look at every step of the way.

  1. Day 1

    Talk

    A free call to understand the problem, the people who'll use the system and what “done” looks like for you.

  2. Within a week

    Scope

    A written proposal: features, screens, timeline and a fixed price. You know exactly what you're getting before any code is written.

  3. Every week

    Build

    Short cycles with a live preview link. You click through real screens, give feedback, and we adjust while it's still cheap to change.

  4. Launch & after

    Launch & look after

    Deployment, data migration and team training, followed by maintenance and support from the people who built it.

05 — Why clients stay

Three rules we
don't bend.

  1. i.

    Built for the field, not the demo.

    Your staff will use this system on a busy day, a slow connection and a cheap phone. We design for that reality, not for a polished sales presentation.

  2. ii.

    Clear scope, honest price.

    A written proposal with a fixed price before we start, and weekly progress you can click through. No surprise invoices, no disappearing developers.

  3. iii.

    We answer for what we ship.

    The people who write your code are the people who support it after launch. You get the source, the documentation, and a team that still picks up the phone.
