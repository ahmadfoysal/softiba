02 — How they connect

Not a portfolio.
A system.

Every product we ship becomes infrastructure for the next one. WhatSwift carries the login codes and alerts for RemoteMikrotik. SwiftNotify uses it to reach WooCommerce customers. Radtik and RemoteMikrotik both talk to the same routers.

That means fixes travel. When we make one piece faster or safer, the whole family gets it.

03 — How we work

Three rules we
don't bend.

  1. i.

    Built for the field, not the demo.

    Our users fix networks at 2 a.m., teach forty students before Fajr, and take orders on a phone between customers. Software has to work on a slow connection and a cheap handset — or it doesn't work.

  2. ii.

    Small tools that talk to each other.

    We'd rather ship six focused products that connect cleanly than one giant one nobody can learn. Each does a single job, and does it properly.

  3. iii.

    We answer for what we ship.

    The people who write the code are the people who answer support. No outsourced helpdesk, no ticket graveyard — support is around the clock because the products are.
